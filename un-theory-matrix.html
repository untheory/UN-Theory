UN Theory® — Master Empirical Benchmark Matrix

This dossier cross-references registered UN Theory derivations against independent astronomical, cosmological, laboratory and geophysical observations. The observations themselves originate from external survey teams, observatories and experimental collaborations. UN Theory does not claim to have produced these measurements. Rather, the purpose of this matrix is to document cases where UN Theory claims to derive first-principles explanations for previously reported observational anomalies, tensions, unexplained correlations or unresolved phenomena, and to identify areas where contemporary observations appear consistent with independently developed substrate-based derivations.
